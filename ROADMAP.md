# Curriculum Roadmap & Milestone Schedule (`v3.1.0`)

This roadmap defines the canonical, strictly acyclic progression through the **Embedded C Master Learning Hub** across **Onboarding (`START_HERE.md` + `LEARNERS/`), 4 engineering phases, 17 logical learning stages (`Step 01`–`Step 17`: `01b` + `02`–`17`), 20 practical laboratories (`Labs 01–20`), 12 reusable bare-metal drivers, 5 mastery examinations (`34` graded questions), and 3 compiled & onboard-hardware-tested capstone firmware projects**.

> **Authoritative Metrics Source (`AUDIT/release_metrics.json`):**  
> `350` Source-of-Truth concepts | `27` domains (`0` DAG cycles) | `17` logical stages | `20` labs (`13` onboard-tested, `6` external hardware pending, `1` host-only) | `12` STM32G474RE bare-metal drivers | `3` capstones (onboard-tested) | `70` live-verified videos (`100%` HTTP 200) | `594/594` host C11 assertions (`96.04%` `gcov -b` line coverage across 10 host-linked drivers) | `0` fabricated physical results.

> **Dependency Order Note:** Per [`01-master-concept-map/overview.md`](01-master-concept-map/overview.md) and [`01-master-concept-map/learning-sequence.md`](01-master-concept-map/learning-sequence.md), **Basic GPIO on reset 16 MHz HSI16 (`08-gpio/`, Step 07, Labs 01–02)** is completed *before* **Dual 170 MHz / 160 MHz RCC PLL Boost (`07-rcc-and-clocks/`, Step 08, Lab 03)**, and **Interrupts/SysTick/NVIC/EXTI (`10-interrupts/`, Step 09, Labs 04–05)** are completed *before* **Hardware Timers & PWM (`09-timers/`, Step 10, Labs 06–07)**.

### Phase 1: Foundations & Bare-Metal Core (`Steps 01–05`: `Stage 01b` + `Stages 02–05`, Level 1–2)
- **Milestone 1.1 — Step 01 (`01b-digital-electronics-and-hw-integration/` — `Stage 01b`):** Ohm's law, RC $\tau$, CMOS thresholds, DS12288 `FT`/`TT_a` & $\pm 20\text{ mA}$ limits, UM2505 `MB1367` schematics (`JP6` IDD vs `JP5`, Active-HIGH `B1` `PC13`, `SB6`), analog rail integrity (`HWSW-ANA-001` `VREFINT_CAL`), and lab instrument SOPs.
- **Milestone 1.2 — Step 02 (`02-c-fundamentals/` — `Stage 02`):** ISO C11 translation pipeline, `<stdint.h>`, two's complement, integer promotions, bitwise masking, pointers, 2D DMA arrays, C99 FAMs, tagged unions, function-pointer jump tables, `<stdatomic.h>`, Newlib `_reent`/`__malloc_lock` (`C-LIB-001`), and passing `tests/test_c_foundations.c` (**`116/116`** host assertions) + Stage 01 Exam.
- **Milestone 1.3 — Step 03 (`03-embedded-c/` — `Stage 03`):** MMIO struct overlays, `volatile` limits, `rc_w1`/`rc_w0`/`w` semantics, `MODIFY_REG`, `DMB`/`DSB`/`ISB` barriers, nested `PRIMASK`/`BASEPRI`, `LDREX`/`STREX` CAS, seqlocks, zero-overhead static HAL dispatch (`FW-DRV-003`), async driver/proxy/HSM patterns, MISRA C:2023 + Stage 02 Exam.
- **Milestone 1.4 — Step 04 (`04-arm-cortex-m/` — `Stage 04`):** ARMv7E-M Cortex-M4F r0p1 pipeline, `R0–R15` (`PC+4`, `bit[0]=0`), `xPSR` (`N,Z,C,V,Q,GE`), `MSP`/`PSP`, Thumb-2, AAPCS hard-float ABI, 8-word vs 26-word FPU frames, `EXC_RETURN`, lazy stacking, `CFSR`/`HFSR`/`MMFAR`/`BFAR`, 8-region MPU, DSP/SIMD (`SMLAD`, `__SSAT`).
- **Milestone 1.5 — Step 05 (`05-toolchain-and-debugging/` — `Stage 05`):** `arm-none-eabi-gcc` + Make/CMake, linker scripts (`FLASH`, `RAM`, `CCMRAM (xrw)` + `_siccmram`, `.noinit`), GNU `ld --wrap` & `FLASH`↔`CCMRAM` veneers (`TOOL-LD-004`), 118-IRQ vector table (`472 B`, `512 B` aligned), STLINK-V3E/OpenOCD/GDB/SVD, `DWT->CYCCNT`, `SWO`/`TPIU->ACPR` (`TOOL-DBG-004`) + Stage 03 Exam.

### Phase 2: Core STM32G474RE Platform Bring-Up (`Steps 06–09`: `Stage 06 [Part A]` → `Stage 07` → `Stage 06 [Part B]` → `Stage 08`, Level 1–2)
- **Milestone 2.1 — Step 06 (`06-stm32-fundamentals/` — `Stage 06 [Part A]`):** Multi-layer AHB BusMatrix (`0x20018000` CCM-SRAM DMA alias, AHB2 `DAC1–4`), LQFP64 52-pin boundaries (`GPIOE`/`SPI4`/`FMC`/`AES` exclusions), RM0440/DS12288/UM2505/ES0430 navigation.
- **Milestone 2.2 — Step 07 (`08-gpio/` — `Stage 07`):** Reset `11b` Analog `MODER`, atomic `BSRR`/`BRR`, `LCKR` (`gpio_g4.c`), `SYSCFG->CFGR1` Fm+ 20 mA (`GPIO-FMP-001`), `PWR_CR3.UCPD_DBDIS` — **Labs 01–02** (`ONBOARD HARDWARE TESTED`; optional scope/manual button checks noted in [`AUDIT/LAB_VERIFICATION_MATRIX.md`](AUDIT/LAB_VERIFICATION_MATRIX.md)).
- **Milestone 2.3 — Step 08 (`07-rcc-and-clocks/` — `Stage 06 [Part B]`):** 24 MHz HSE `X3`, PLL bounds ($[2.66, 16]\text{ MHz}$ / $[96, 344]\text{ MHz}$), `PWR_SR2.VOSF`, `PWR_CR5 @ 0x80` Range 1 Boost, Flash 4WS + ART, mandatory RM0440 §6.1.5 `HPRE=/2` ($\ge 1\ \mu\text{s}$) ramp, **dual `170 MHz` & `160 MHz` Boost profiles** (`clock_g4.c`) — **Lab 03** (`ONBOARD HARDWARE TESTED`; external `PA8` MCO scope check optional).
- **Milestone 2.4 — Step 09 (`10-interrupts/` — `Stage 08`):** 24-bit SysTick, 4-bit NVIC `PRIGROUP`, `SYSCFG->EXTICR`, Active-HIGH `EXTI->RTSR1`, direct `EXTI->PR1 = mask` + `(void)EXTI->PR1;` + `__DSB()` (`exti_g4.c`) — **Labs 04–05** (`ONBOARD HARDWARE TESTED`).
