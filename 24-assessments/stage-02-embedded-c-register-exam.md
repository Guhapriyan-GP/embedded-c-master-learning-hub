**Aligned Stage:** Stage 02 (`03-embedded-c/`)

**Total Points:** 100 | **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 85$ Points | **Answer Key:** [`answer-keys-and-rubrics.md`](answer-keys-and-rubrics.md#stage-02-answer-key--rubric)

### Questions (100 Points Total)

1. **Volatile Semantics & Optimizer Elimination (20 Points):**
   Consider `while ((*status_reg & 0x01U) == 0U) { }`. Compare the Thumb-2 assembly emitted at `-O2` when `status_reg` is `uint32_t *` versus `volatile uint32_t *`. Why does `volatile` alone NOT guarantee atomicity or hardware bus ordering?

2. **Read-Modify-Write (RMW) Race Condition on `ODR` (15 Points):**
   Trace the exact 3-instruction Thumb-2 sequence generated by `GPIOA->ODR |= (1U << 5U);` in `main()`. Demonstrate step-by-step how an ISR executing `GPIOA->ODR |= (1U << 6U);` between the load and store silently corrupts bit 6.

3. **Atomic Pin Manipulation (`BSRR` / `BRR`) & Bit-Banding Exclusion (15 Points):**
   (a) Explain why `GPIOA->BSRR = GPIO_BSRR_BS5;` and `GPIOA->BRR = GPIO_BRR_BR5;` are single-instruction atomic writes and why writing `0` bits has no effect.
   (b) Why can STM32G474RE `GPIOA` (`0x48000000`) **NOT** be manipulated via Cortex-M4 hardware peripheral bit-banding?

4. **Hardware Flag Clear Semantics (`rc_w1`, `rc_w0`, WO) (20 Points):**
   Identify and fix the exact hardware register bug in each of the three STM32G474RE statements below:
   - (a) `EXTI->PR1 |= EXTI_PR1_PIF13;` (`rc_w1`)
   - (b) `TIM2->SR &= ~TIM_SR_UIF;` (`rc_w0`)
   - (c) `TIM2->EGR |= TIM_EGR_UG;` (`WO`)
   - (d) `ADC1->ISR |= ADC_ISR_ADRDY;` (`rc_w1`)

5. **CMSIS Memory-Mapped Struct Chain Derivation (15 Points):**
   Starting from `PERIPH_BASE = 0x40000000UL` and `AHB2PERIPH_BASE = PERIPH_BASE + 0x08000000UL`, write the complete CMSIS `#define` chain for `GPIOA` and `GPIOC` (stride `0x400`) as pointers to `GPIO_TypeDef`, and explain why CMSIS marks every struct member `__IO` (`volatile`).

6. **Reentrant Critical Sections & Memory Barriers (15 Points):**
   Write a nested/reentrant CMSIS critical section pair (`crit_enter()` / `crit_exit()`) using `__get_PRIMASK()`, `__disable_irq()`, `__set_PRIMASK()`, and compiler/memory barrier intrinsics (`__DMB()`). Explain why blindly calling `__enable_irq()` at exit breaks nested critical sections.
